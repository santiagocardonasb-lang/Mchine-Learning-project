Detecting drift and performance degradation

  • Rate drift: population stability index of the monthly report rate across UPZ, last complete month vs training months, ten quantile bins; flag above {{ config.DRIFT_PSI_THRESHOLD }} (a significant shift).
  • Mix drift: largest change in the share of any waste type, size or vector category; flag above {{ "%.0f"|format(config.DRIFT_SHARE_THRESHOLD * 100) }} points.
  • Degradation: live macro-F1 of the last predicted month below {{ "%.0f"|format(config.PERFORMANCE_DROP_FRACTION * 100) }} % of the cross-validation macro-F1 of the active model (concept drift; Gama et al., 2014).

Any flag shows a retraining recommendation and a Retrain now button on the monitoring page.

Retrain when: a drift or performance flag appears; {{ config.AUTO_RETRAIN_THRESHOLD }} new citizen reports accumulate (automatic); the features or the data sources change; or the active model is more than three months old.

Risks, quality controls and update procedure

  • Quality controls: the automated test suite runs before every merge (leakage, complete months, cut points from training months, inference logging, drift calculations); moderation keeps false reports out of training.
  • Risks: reporting bias across zones, a sudden campaign that inflates reports, data from the city changing format, and a retrain on too little real data.
  • Update procedure: (1) change code or parameters on a branch and run scripts/crisp_evidence.py and the tests; (2) merge through a pull request; Render redeploys; (3) retrain from the admin pages; (4) check the new run on the model and monitoring pages; (5) older runs keep their metrics, predictions and logs for traceability.
