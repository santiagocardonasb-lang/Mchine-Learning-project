Metrics chosen for the business question, temporal cross-validation, overfitting and bias analysis, expert review and the final result on months the model never saw.

Metrics and why

  • Macro-F1 (main metric): the average F1 of the three classes, so the high class counts as much as the others; it is the selection criterion.
  • Recall of the high class: share of the zones that really were high that the model flagged; it breaks ties.
  • Accuracy: reported for reference only, because a model can score well on it while missing the high zones.
  • Confusion matrix: shows which errors happen, especially high zones predicted as low.

Temporal cross-validation and comparison

Five-fold TimeSeriesSplit over whole months: each fold trains on the past and validates on the following months, as the model is used in practice (Bergmeir & Benítez, 2012). Random K-fold would mix future and past months.

Overfitting, underfitting, bias and risks

  • Overfitting: deep trees reach a macro-F1 near 1.0 on the training folds but do not validate better. The selected forest (depth {{ config.MAX_DEPTH }}) keeps the gap small: {{ "%.2f"|format(ev.best.random_forest.train_macro_f1) }} on training folds vs {{ "%.2f"|format(ev.best.random_forest.val_macro_f1) }} on validation.
  • Underfitting: the baseline ({{ "%.2f"|format(fm.cv.baseline.macro_f1.mean) }}) and the linear model ({{ "%.2f"|format(fm.cv.logistic_regression.macro_f1.mean) }}) stay below the forest, so non-linear effects matter.
  • Ceiling of the problem: each month adds random variation that no feature can foresee. A model that knew the exact parameters of the data generator would reach about 0.63 macro-F1 on this target; the selected model reaches {{ "%.2f"|format(fm.cv.random_forest.macro_f1.mean) }}, about {{ "%.0f"|format(fm.cv.random_forest.macro_f1.mean / 0.632 * 100) }} % of that ceiling.
  • Medium class: the hardest, because it sits between the other two; most errors are between neighboring levels.
  • Bias: zones where citizens report less would look safer than they are; rates per inhabitant and the territorial features reduce, but do not remove, this effect. UPZ population is estimated by area.
  • Synthetic data: the metrics show that the method works on data calibrated on the real city, not yet its accuracy on real reports; live monitoring measures that as real months close.

Review with a domain-knowledgeable person

The results and the system were reviewed with the course professor of Machine Learning in the project reviews; the feedback on the presentation of the system is documented in docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-07-professor-feedback-design.md. The professor asked for visible model parameters, cross-validation mean ± standard deviation, a comparison against a baseline and a real error analysis, and for the project context to come before the tools.
