What it means

The model expects {{ result.upz.name }} ({{ result.upz.locality.name }}) to be at {{ result.level }} risk in {{ result.period.strftime("%B") }}, with {{ (result.probability * 100) | round | int }}% of its vote on that level.

Levels compare UPZ by reports per 10,000 inhabitants in a month: low is up to {{ "%.1f" | format(m.cuts[0]) }}, medium up to {{ "%.1f" | format(m.cuts[1]) }}, high above that. These are the terciles of the months this model learned from.

How to use it

High risk means this zone should be early in next month's collection and inspection rounds; medium means watch it; low means no extra action.

{% if current_user.is_admin %}After each training run every high-risk UPZ also becomes a collection priority to schedule.{% else %}Reporting new waste points here helps the next check see what is happening.{% endif %}

Limitations

The model learned from a synthetic report history plus citizen reports, so it reflects where people report, not every pile in the city. On its test months it scored a macro F1 of {{ "%.2f" | format(m.test.macro_f1) }} and found {{ (m.test.recall_high * 100) | round | int }}% of the high-risk zone-months.

The percentage is the model's confidence, not a guarantee. Use it with local knowledge and the latest reports on the map.
