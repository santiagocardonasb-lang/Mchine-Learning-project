How tickets are handled

Incident: the app or a page stopped working, or data looks wrong (a report in the wrong zone, a risk that makes no sense).

Bug: something works but behaves unexpectedly.

Suggestion: an idea to make LocWaste more useful.

Every ticket starts open. An administrator moves it to in review while looking into it and to resolved with an answer, which you see below.
